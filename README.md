# Meshage Community

Welcome to the official issue tracker and discussion board for the **Meshage** app (available at [meshage.me](https://meshage.me)).

## How to Use This Repo
* **Issues:** Report bugs or submit specific feature requests.
* **Discussions:** Ask questions, share ideas, and connect with the community.

## About Meshage
Meshage is a multi-mesh, mobile-first messaging app supporting both MeshCore and Reticulum. Meshage tames the multiple-mesh, many-identity, several-nodes-per-person reality with a Unified Person view and Verified Identity Claiming in a simplified, person-first UI.

With a custom mobile-first Nomad browser and support for RRC Hubs and MeshCore Room Servers, users can engage fully with their mesh community.

Meshage is free (as in beer) and eagerly contributes back to the open-source foundations it stands upon.

## About the Author
Meshage is built by an independent developer and mesh networking enthusiast who believes everyone has the right to communication that is private, resilient, and free from gatekeepers. Choose your mesh, choose your name ... that's it. No tracking, no centralized infrastructure, just end-to-end encrypted messaging.
